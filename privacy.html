Privacy Policy – Samaj Directory

Samaj Directory is a private member directory for the Rajpurohit samaj community, run by community volunteers.

What we collect

Name, father's and mother's name, village, gotra, mobile number, pincode, city, state, blood group, occupation, and your Google account email and name used to sign in.

How it is used

Only to let signed-in community members find and contact each other (for example by city, occupation or blood group), and for community admins to manage the directory. We never sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who can see it

Other signed-in members see your directory profile. Your mobile number is shown only if you allow it. Community admins can see all profile data.

Your choices

You can edit your profile or hide your mobile number in the app at any time. To delete your data, contact the admins below.
